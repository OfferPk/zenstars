## Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
Immerse yourself in ZenStars, a tranquil and sleek offline Star Battle (Queens / Two Not Touch) celestial logic puzzle designed to quiet your mind and heighten your deductive focus.

Gaze upon radiant cosmic grids divided into delicate constellation zones. Your objective is pure, geometric, and profoundly satisfying: place glowing celestial stars across the celestial coordinates such that every row, every column, and every colored constellation zone contains the exact required number of stars (1 or 2 stars), while ensuring that no two stars ever touch—not even diagonally.

With no timers, no pressure, no ads, and zero network requirements, ZenStars is a peaceful, mindful sanctuary where classic logic puzzle deduction meets celestial elegance.
