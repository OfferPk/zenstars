How to Play

✦

Exact Quota

Every row, column, and colored constellation zone must contain the exact required number of stars (usually 1).

🚫

No Touching Stars

Stars cannot be adjacent to each other in any direction—horizontally, vertically, or diagonally (King's move).

👆

Cell Controls

Tap a cell to cycle: Empty ➔ Dot (Ruled Out) ➔ Star ➔ Empty. You can also drag across cells to quickly paint dots!

💡

Mindful Assistance

Use Hint whenever you seek deduction inspiration, and freely undo any step with unlimited undo.
